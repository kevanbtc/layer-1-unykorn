# 🗺️ Unykorn L1 - Development Roadmap

> **Vision**: Building the most enterprise-ready, scalable, and developer-friendly Layer-1 blockchain for global financial institutions and real-world asset tokenization.

---

## 🎯 Strategic Objectives

1. **Enterprise Adoption**: Become the preferred L1 for financial institutions and RWA tokenization
2. **Developer Experience**: Provide best-in-class tools, documentation, and support
3. **Security First**: Maintain highest security standards with regular audits
4. **Performance**: Achieve >1000 TPS with sub-second finality
5. **Ecosystem Growth**: Build a thriving community of developers and validators
